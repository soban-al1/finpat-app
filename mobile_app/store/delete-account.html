Delete your account

Two ways to permanently remove your FinPat account and data.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FinPat and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Actions, tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account and data are erased immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't sign in, email privacy@finpat.app from the address on your FinPat account with the subject “Delete my account”.

We may ask one question to confirm you own the account. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

This cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no recovery window and no way to restore your history afterwards. If you only want to start over, use Settings → Reset All Data instead, which clears your records but keeps your account.

Retention

Your data is removed from our live systems immediately. Encrypted backups are purged on a rolling cycle of no more than 90 days, after which no copy remains. We keep no residual account data beyond what the law requires of us.
